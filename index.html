Abstract摘要

Pixel art is widely used in games and digital content creation, requiring readable forms, coherent spatial relationships, and harmonious colors under limited resolution and palette constraints. Language models show growing promise in visual creation, yet their pixel art creation capabilities lack systematic evaluation. We introduce PixelBench, formalizing text-to-image generation and instruction-guided editing for pixel art and providing a benchmark for these tasks. We further present a baseline approach in which language model agents invoke drawing tools through code to generate and edit pixel art. We assess the quality of generated artwork through human evaluation and employ a multimodal large language model as a judge, validating the agreement between its automated assessments and human preferences. We compare language models and configurations across diverse subjects and canvas sizes. In our experiments, GPT-6 Astra achieves both the highest average judge score and the best average human rank, highlighting the potential of general-purpose language models for pixel art creation. PixelBench brings together task definitions, a creation method, and evaluation protocols in a unified framework for studying pixel art generation and editing with language models.

像素画广泛用于游戏和数字内容创作，其制作要求在有限分辨率和调色板下组织清晰的形态、连贯的空间关系与协调的色彩。语言模型在视觉创作中展现出新的潜力，但其像素画创作能力仍缺乏系统评估。我们提出 PixelBench，规范化文本生成像素画与指令驱动编辑两类任务，并构建相应的评测基准。我们进一步提供一种基于语言模型 Agent 的像素画创作基础方法，通过代码调用绘图工具完成像素画的生成与编辑。在评价方面，我们通过人类评价衡量生成作品的质量，并采用多模态大语言模型作为评审，并验证了其自动评价结果与人类偏好的一致性。我们在多种题材与画幅上比较了不同语言模型及其配置，实验中 GPT-6 Astra 获得最高 Judge 评分和最高人类平均名次，显示出通用语言模型用于像素画创作的潜力。 PixelBench 将任务定义、创作方法与评价协议结合起来，为系统研究语言模型的像素画生成与编辑能力提供了统一框架。

1Task suite任务套件

75 text-to-image tasks across 7 categories — items, characters, scenes, creatures, materials, effects, UI — at canvas sizes from 16×16 to 256×256, plus a 25-task instruction-guided editing suite coming soon.

75 道文生图题目，覆盖 7 个类别 —— 物品、人物、场景、生物、材质、特效、UI —— 画幅从 16×16 到 256×256，另有 25 道指令驱动编辑题目。

2Agentic creation baselineAgent 创作基线

The model draws inside a persistent canvas REPL: it registers a palette, calls drawing primitives, inspects the render, and iterates. Every call is traced, so process quality — not just the final image — is measurable.

模型在持久画布 REPL 中作画：注册调色板、调用绘图原语、观察渲染结果、迭代修改。所有调用都被完整记录，因此「过程质量」而不仅是最终图像都可被度量。

3Evaluation protocol评价协议

A multimodal LLM judge scores every image 0–100 on padded, nearest-neighbour upscaled inputs; four human raters independently rank all 13 models per task. The two orderings agree closely.

多模态 LLM 评审在补边 + 最近邻放大后的输入上为每张图打 0–100 分；4 位人类评审对每题 13 个模型独立排序。两种排序高度一致。

    Task suite 2任务套件 2 — instruction-guided editing, in preparation 指令驱动的编辑，筹备中

    Editing an existing pixel-art asset修改已有的像素画资产

    The second half of PixelBench asks a different question: given a finished pixel-art asset and a written instruction, can a model make exactly the requested change and leave everything else alone? The model sees the asset and the instruction but never the reference result, and its whole session is traced like the creation tasks. 25 tasks are being prepared; results will appear here.

    PixelBench 的另一半问的是另一个问题：给一张已完成的像素画资产和一句文字指令，模型能否只做出被要求的那处修改、而不动其它部分？模型能看到资产与指令，但看不到参考结果，整个过程同样被完整记录。25 道题正在准备中，结果将在此处更新。

    What a task looks like题目形态

    An input asset, an editing instruction, and the output constraints it must still satisfy (canvas size, colour budget, transparency). Some tasks ask for a short frame sequence rather than a single image, so a change in viewing angle or pose can be judged over time.

    一张输入资产、一句编辑指令，以及输出仍须满足的约束（画幅、颜色预算、透明度）。部分题目要求输出一小段帧序列，以便按时间判断视角或姿态的变化。

    What will be reported将报告什么

    Whether the requested change actually happened, whether edits stayed inside the permitted region, and whether the untouched parts of the asset survived. Both failure directions matter: doing nothing and damaging unrelated regions are reported separately, because preservation alone would reward an agent that never edits.

    被要求的修改是否真的发生、改动是否落在允许区域内、未涉及的部位是否被保住。两个方向的失败会分开报告：什么都不改、以及改对了却破坏了无关区域——只强调保持会奖励「不编辑」的模型。

    Evaluation评价方式

    Deterministic checks for the instruction's target and its locality, paired with human judgement of whether the edit follows the instruction, looks natural, and leaves a usable asset. The same judge protocol as the creation suite is used for scale, with its agreement to human preference reported.

    对指令目标与改动范围使用确定性检查，并由人类判断编辑是否遵循指令、结果是否自然、资产是否仍可用。规模化部分沿用与生成套件相同的 judge 协议，并报告其与人类偏好的一致性。

    Results评测结果

    13 models × 75 tasks = 975 artworks. Every artwork was scored by a multimodal LLM judge (gemini-3.8-flash, 0–100) and ranked by four human raters, who ordered all 13 models for each of the 75 tasks. GPT-6 Astra is first on both metrics.

    13 个模型 × 75 道题 = 975 张作品。每张作品都由多模态 LLM 评审（gemini-3.8-flash，0–100 分）打分，并由 4 位人类评审对每题 13 个模型排序。GPT-6 Astra 在两项指标上均排名第一。

      Protocol评测协议 — how these numbers were produced 这些数字是如何得到的

      LLM-as-judgeLLM 评审

      • Judge model gemini-3.8-flash; one image per call, a single 0–100 number requested, temperature 0.2.
      • 评审模型 gemini-3.8-flash；每次调用一张图，只要求输出一个 0–100 的数字，temperature 0.2。
      • Inputs are the native canvas, given a transparent margin (width/16 left, height/16 top) and then nearest-neighbour upscaled to 512×512 — the padding removes grid alignment that depressed scores, and the upscale adds no anti-aliasing.
      • 输入为原始分辨率画布，先加透明留白（左 width/16、上 height/16），再用最近邻放大到 512×512 —— 留白消除了压低分数的网格对齐效应，放大过程不引入抗锯齿。
      • 975 cells = 75 tasks × 13 models, no failures; scores span 15–96.
      • 975 个单元格 = 75 题 × 13 模型，无失败；分数范围 15–96。

      Human evaluation人类评价

      • Four raters independently ordered all 13 models for each of the 75 tasks (a full ranking per task, not a 1–5 score).
      • 4 位评审对 75 道题分别独立排序 13 个模型（每题一个完整排序，而非 1–5 打分）。
      • Mean pairwise Spearman agreement between raters is 0.670 — the humans disagree with each other about as much as the judge disagrees with the human mean.
      • 评审两两之间的平均 Spearman 一致性为 0.670 ——人类彼此的分歧与 Judge 和人类均值之间的分歧相当。
      • Reported as the mean 1-based rank over raters × tasks (7.0 = no preference signal).
      • 报告值为评审 × 题目的平均名次（1 起始，7.0 表示无偏好信号）。

      Generation setting生成设置

      • Every model draws through the same agentic REPL surface with an identical tool budget, temperature and prompt, so differences come from the model, not the harness.
      • 所有模型使用同一套 Agent REPL 工具面、相同的工具预算、temperature 与提示词，差异来自模型本身而非 harness。
      • Palette size is capped by each task (max_colors, 86 in the suite); the canvas starts fully transparent and is rendered without anti-aliasing.
      • 调色板数量由题目约束（max_colors，本套题为 86）；画布初始全透明，渲染全程无抗锯齿。
